# PHP2Shell vulnerabilities

Cutoff: October 22, 2026, America/New_York.

The PHP project's October 21 advisory identifies CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 as critical flaws that can allow unauthenticated remote code execution. It lists fixed releases 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 and 8.2.35. The advance announcement rates both CVSS v4.0 9.2. [PHP release archive](https://www.php.net/archive/2026.php), [security-team notice](https://news-web.php.net/php.announce/509)

The widely reported roughly 70% figure describes PHP's website prevalence. It does not establish that 70% of the internet was hacked, compromised or uniformly exploitable. The exact prevalence denominator and deployment conditions matter. Keep confirmed exploitation, attempted exploitation, affected software and preventive outages separate.
