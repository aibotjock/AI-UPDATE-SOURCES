## Reuse and updating

Retain this dated snapshot. Add future dated research directories and link corrections from the affected company. Distinguish demonstrated availability, company announcements, independent measurements and editorial forecasts. Every numeric benchmark record needs exact model/configuration, metric/version, publisher, date and limitations. Unknown is not zero. Never promote the October 12 repo charts to October 22 standings. Keep full publisher articles outside this distributable research library.

## Editorial scope

AI UPDATE is a news report. Prioritize verified developments, current capabilities, model standings, major deals and pertinent valuations. Omit routine tariffs, investment recommendations, return calculations and profitability speculation. Do not fill evidence gaps with financial commentary.
