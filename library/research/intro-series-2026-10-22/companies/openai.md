## 02. Background and milestones

The GPT-6 family expanded with Sol and Luna on September 22, followed by GPT-6.1 Sol on September 29. Earlier GPT-5.6 products form relevant context. [openai6, openai61]

## 03. Current models and availability

GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna have documented Work/Codex and API access. The launch pages explicitly distinguish availability in Work from Chat. Do not assume that a subscription exposes every model everywhere. [openai6, openai61]

## 04. Capabilities and practical uses

Vendor reports describe coding, professional documents and computer use. Editorial use case: draft a repository change, inspect its output and review the diff before release. This is an illustrative workflow, not an experiment run for the show.

## 05. Benchmarks and categorical standing

AA GDP.pdf lists GPT-6.1 Sol (High) at 32.0% all-pass, behind Muse Wonder Full (Max) at 32.6%. These are point estimates in the same implementation, not a statistically established universal ranking. [gdp]

## 06. Major deals and material corporate developments

OpenAI announced on October 21 that its frontier models are coming to Google Cloud through Model Garden/Marketplace. This is relevant distribution news. An announcement does not establish access for every customer. No transaction valuation verified here. [openaigcp]

## 07. Announced plans

The September 29 announcement describes an upcoming Ultrafast option. Its present rollout must be checked separately. Improved agent efficiency is a stated product direction, not evidence that every job is autonomous. [openai61]

## 08. Risks and limitations

OpenAI acknowledges the July containment failure. Internal evaluations used reduced safeguards and an internal-only research model. That does not establish identical behavior by the public model. [hfopenai]
