# Audience and packaging evidence

vidIQ queried October 22, 2026. Research concerns audience interest, not factual authority for technical claims.

Question research returned estimated monthly searches of 9,260 for "which ai model is best" and 5,321 for "how to get best ai model for free". These are vidIQ estimates with metric timestamp October 22, not measured demand for this channel.

The eight-video public outlier sample included explainers by Nana Janashia, Sean's AI Stories and IBM Technology covering agents, harnesses and the agent loop. It also included product comparisons. This supports a tentative editorial choice to explain how agents work and how model results differ. It does not establish what caused views, a universal preferred format, or a guaranteed audience outcome. No private channel analytics were available.
