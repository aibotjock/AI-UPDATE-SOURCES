EDITORIAL QUESTION AND VIEWER BENEFIT

Help viewers answer: “What does a leaderboard result actually tell me about choosing a model for my work and budget?” Teach a reusable decision sequence:
1. Match the evaluation to the task.
2. Identify the exact model and complete tested configuration, including tools, effort, and attempts.
3. Check measurement date, benchmark version, coverage, and evidence type.
4. Consider uncertainty and whether a difference matters.
5. Compare actual task success and total cost.

Explain the sequence through a concrete example rather than reciting a catalog of acronyms. Distinguish capability, human preference, factual reliability, system/tool performance, speed, and value. Published scores and channel-run demonstrations are different evidence.
