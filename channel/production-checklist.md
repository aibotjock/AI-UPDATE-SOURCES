# Episode quality and acceptance checklist — version 2

Use the selected delivery stage. Mark pass only for performed checks, otherwise pending, failed or not applicable with a reason.

## Research and accuracy
- Fresh research precedes factual narration; actual dates/cutoff recorded.
- Original sources and maintenance evidence inspected; each material claim matches evidence.
- Report/event/measurement/capture dates separated; settings/availability checked.
- Company claims, independent findings, tests, forecasts and unknowns differentiated.
- Numbers/labels/qualifiers agree across narration, visuals and reference records.
- Incident attribution, attempted versus confirmed activity and relevant responses preserved.
- Same-origin coverage is not counted as independent corroboration.
- Routine financial coverage excluded; major deals/valuations included only when pertinent.

## Learning, organization and attention
- One central question and three specific valuable learning outcomes.
- Prerequisites briefly explained; depth progresses without assuming inaccessible prior knowledge.
- Selected segments serve this episode; unnecessary segments omitted.
- Concrete examples and evidence support explanations.
- Opening promise resolved; closing takeaway demonstrates what the viewer learned.
- Scene changes follow meaning; source reading holds are sufficient.
- No invented claims about audience retention, views or production gains.

## Visuals, sources and thumbnails
- Distinct backgrounds/compositions, suitable imagery and purposeful motion.
- Recolored repeated card layouts do not stand in for the requested improvement.
- Important sources appear during discussion, with readable publisher/title/date and exact highlights.
- Context retained; originals separate from overlays; references link to original URLs.
- Charts preserve units/scales/uncertainty and derive from verified data.
- Thumbnails use truthful strong hierarchy, relevant large subjects and small-screen-readable text.
- Asset provenance/reuse checked; illustrative/generated media never posed as evidence.
- Full-screen and reduced-size visual review completed.

## Real browser checks for ready-to-record delivery
- Open the delivered package in a real browser; record environment/version.
- Navigate all scenes/reveals; test keyboard after toolbar interaction.
- Click the visible Fullscreen button, exit, re-enter by shortcut and inspect state/error handling.
- Clean capture hides controls and notes and remains recoverable.
- Source zoom/highlight overlays match narration/evidence.
- Draw, erase, undo/redo, clear/undo and export/import scene ink.
- Verify annotation alignment before/after resize and fullscreen.
- Play real embedded clips; test pause/replay, loading and fallbacks.
- Verify teleprompter cues and accurately describe synchronization limits.
- Ensure packaged assets load without missing paths, clipped text or hidden access errors.
- Simulated DOM tests and static SVG renders recorded separately; neither counts as playback.

## OBS and finished-video checks when requested
- Actual Linux screen/window capture, microphone and webcam tested where available.
- Guides/controls/teleprompter excluded from recorded frame.
- Actual narration and measured runtime; 15–30 minutes for introductory series.
- Final captions/audio/visuals synchronized; citations readable.
- Complete audiovisual playback performed and issues resolved.
- If OBS/hardware unavailable, report that limitation; do not claim tested recording.
- Publishing and paid commitments require user authorization.

## Continuity and handoff
- Player, full script, source links/captures, manifest, ledger, selected media and instructions present for ready-to-record delivery.
- A finished video additionally contains actual recorded narration and final playback evidence.
- Reusable resources, corrections and company files updated with matching organization.
- Historical material preserved; no secrets or unrelated personal data.
- No VidIQ call without explicit permission for this task, even for free/status operations.
