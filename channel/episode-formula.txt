EPISODE FORMULA — AI Update with Chris Gawad M.D., M.Sc.
Version 2.0.0 · October 24, 2026

PURPOSE
A recognizable news and learning format with flexible segment selection. Every episode has a central question, three valuable learning outcomes, accurate evidence, distinct visuals and a concrete takeaway. Do not require all segments or paid tests in each episode.

DURATION
Introductory series: three to five videos, each 15–30 minutes. Aim for approximately 22–28 minutes when content warrants. Regular updates retain the earlier 10–15-minute target unless the user or episode brief changes it. Duration is established through rehearsal/recording, not only word count. Do not pad.

SEGMENT MENU
- The Download: consequential verified developments and the episode's central question.
- Under the Hood: accessible explanations and relationships needed to understand the news.
- Benchmark Breakdown: relevant compatible evaluations, methodology and limitations.
- Prompt vs. Prompt: genuine documented output comparisons when useful and authorized.
- The Takeaway: what changed, why it matters, what the audience learned and remaining uncertainty.
- Next on the Radar: announced milestones and evidence-backed questions to revisit; forecasts labeled.
Use short intro/outro branding and selected chapter identifiers. Choose, combine or omit menu items; explain choices in the episode brief. An opening learning promise and meaningful closing learning takeaway are essential, but separate named chapters are not compulsory. How-to lessons remain occasional for best/new items.

ILLUSTRATIVE 25-MINUTE PLAN — NOT A REQUIRED TEMPLATE
00:00–00:05 Intro
00:05–05:00 The Download
05:00–13:00 Under the Hood
13:00–20:00 Benchmark Breakdown OR Prompt vs. Prompt when useful
20:00–23:00 The Takeaway
23:00–24:55 Next on the Radar when useful
24:55–25:00 Outro
A source-led incident episode could instead combine The Download and Under the Hood, omit model comparisons entirely and give more time to source passages and explanatory diagrams. Record the actual selected plan.

LEARNING PROGRESSION
Proposed five-part spine: who is building what; what systems can do; agents and permissions; platforms and infrastructure; evaluating frontier progress. Select three to five coherent episodes with increasingly demanding applications of earlier concepts. Briefly explain essential terms for new viewers. Do not force each company into every video.

EVIDENCE AND SCREEN PRESENTATION
Research before narration. Use dated original articles, official documentation, research and benchmark sources. Display important sources while discussed: publisher/title/date → relevant passage → timed highlight/zoom → explanatory consequence. Preserve original captures and separate annotations. Full links go in the references and prepared description.
Retain separate report/event/measurement/capture dates, exact conditions and attribution. Reused evidence retains original dates and must be checked for current applicability.

VISUAL AND PLAYER FORMAT
Use Custom Browser Scene Player plus OBS with a clearly visible Fullscreen button, recoverable clean capture, scene/reveal controls, annotations and matching teleprompter cues. Every scene needs a distinct composition and background with consistent brand typography. Pair vivid, polished imagery with calm readable evidence holds. Do not substitute a recolored repeated card layout for the requested visual upgrade.
Thumbnail direction follows the supplied reference photo's focal hierarchy, strong contrast, selective vivid lighting, recognizable subjects and short bold text. Article headlines and thumbnail slogans are not verified facts merely because they appear in a reference image.

WORKFLOW
Master specification → fresh research/evidence packet → dated episode brief → storyboard/narration → source captures and scene production → actual browser rehearsal → OBS recording/post when requested → full audiovisual QA.
A prompt is not a finished episode. Legacy channel/generate_episode.py and its validator are not version-2 compliant; use the new brief template until separately updated.
